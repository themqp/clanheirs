Who We Are

A heritage of trade.
A vision for tomorrow.

ClanHeirs was founded on a simple conviction: that the world's most dynamic economies — China's manufacturing power, West Africa's rising consumer markets, and North America's distribution depth — deserve a trade partner built on trust, speed, and local insight.

We are more than an importer. We are a bridge between cultures, a connector of supply chains, and a committed partner in the growth of businesses across Ghana, Nigeria, the United States, and Canada — sourcing quality merchandise directly from China's most reliable producers.

Our name reflects our ethos: the heirs of a global clan — one family of commerce, spanning oceans and cultures.

What We Do

End-to-end trade solutions

From factory floor to your door, ClanHeirs manages every step of the importation journey — so you can focus on growing your business.

Product Sourcing
We identify and negotiate with the right manufacturers in China, ensuring quality standards and competitive factory pricing for your category.
Freight & Shipping
Sea and air freight solutions tailored to your timeline and budget, with full tracking from port of origin to destination.
Customs & Compliance
Expert handling of import documentation, tariff classification, and customs clearance across all our operating countries.
